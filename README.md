# Reverse-Engineering-the-Perfect-Sinker
https://medium.com/@colinhofmeister/reverse-engineering-the-perfect-sinker-b5fbcbe06f2e

The Seam-Shifted Wake (SSW) effect has emerged as a primary driver of elite sinkers in Major League Baseball, enabling pitches to deviate significantly from trajectories predicted solely by the Magnus effect. However, due to the unique release mechanics and seam orientations required, developing an effective SSW sinker remains a challenge for many pitchers. This paper proposes a data-driven framework to identify pitchers equipped with the prerequisite biomechanics to successfully add an elite sinker to their arsenal. Operating on the hypothesis that the release mechanics required for an SSW sinker closely mirror those of an SSW changeup, an XGBoost classification model was trained on 2025 pitch data. To ensure accurate comparisons across pitch types, the model evaluated spatial delivery characteristics, such as release height, extension, and spin axis, while excluding velocity and spin rate. By applying this model to pitchers who throw a changeup but lack a sinker, the study identified 16 prime candidates who demonstrated both high biometric fit (model score > 60%) and aerodynamic proof of concept (existing changeup SSW deviation > 15°). A retrospective validation using 2024 data confirmed the model's predictive validity: three of the five recommended pitchers successfully added a sinker in 2025, with all three generating positive Run Values. Ultimately, this methodology demonstrates that a pitcher's existing changeup kinematics can reliably predict their aptitude for developing a productive sinker, providing a targeted roadmap for pitch design and arsenal expansion.
